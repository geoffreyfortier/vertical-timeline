Aurikesh Timeline

Preface

The current year is 1567 KR, dating from the beginning of Kovichir's Reign. The veytikka of Balioth assuredly have their own dating system, but they seem generally inclined to either use the Sestomeran dating system or date things from the arrival of Sestomerans to Balioth. Many events from 1359 to 1567 KR, gathered by Syrell Silversluck and are available at the guildhall of the Gallant Shields.

  • First Contact

    -1239 KR: Humans and veytikka meet kagandi for the first time, although all of these races appear in the myths of the others.

    -1239 KR

  • Peace Breaks

    Approx -560 KR: The extended peace between the humans and the kagandi ends in the War of Fallen Towers.

    ~ -560 KR

  • War of Fallen Towers Ends

    -460 KR: The War of Fallen Towers ends in a lasting peace. Many humans now live in domains ruled by kagandi, and vice versa. This is the last time that humans and kagandi fight for reasons of race. Part of the war's end (now forgotten outside of ancient texts) comes from angelic vistation, as the messengers of the gods conclusively show the major religious leaders of the world that the gods recognize humans, kagandi, and veytikka as equals.

    -460 KR

  • Immortal Emperor Unites Sestomera

    1 KR: The Immortal Emperor Kovichir unites the warring domains of Sestomera, as he marries an Imperial Princess (and heir apparent) of one rival. Another rival, exhausted and near collapse from generations of warfare, accepts a position on the High Council in exchange for bending the knee. A decade after his death, that rival is canonized as a saint of Talend, for choosing his obligations to his subjects over his own hubris.

    1 KR

  • Coming of the Istrans

    820 KR: Humans from the continent of Istra, who have previously explored Balioth in small numbers, come to settle the eastern coast (present-day Tyrema and Kaldeshar). They contend with the Gorgrom Empire for territory throughout the southeast.

    820 KR

  • The Battle of Three Legends

    833 KR: Final battle between King Oromec, the Green Man, and the Nightwalker. Oromec receives a mortal wound, and the Green Man is beheaded. Warmaster Chisise becomes the new King of the Gorgrom empire.

    833 KR

  • Fall of the Gorgrom

    841 KR: An uprising in Khar-he-khem ends the Gorgrom Empire. The necromancer Vardevain summons the vengeful souls of those the Empire has oppressed and murdered. Many of the Gorgrom nobles are slain and bound as ghosts. The Gorgrom royal family escapes.

    841 KR

  • Sovereign Knights of Dyvarach Founded

    895 KR: Zereysa creates Zereysa's Star and founds the Sovereign Knights of Dyvarach (a Sestomeran province).

    895 KR

  • Immortal Emperor Abdicates

    976 KR: The Immortal Emperor abdicates the throne of Sestomera, ending some 49 generations of rule. There have been sightings of him since, but none of them have been conclusively confirmed. He is believed to wander the world in disguise, but he can be recognized (so they say) by his glowing green eyes. (He is a kagandi.)

    976 KR

  • The Istran Exodus

    The Istran colonists build new ships and depart for their homeland to settle a succession crisis there, leaving only their dead behind.

    1286 KR

  • First Arrival in Balioth

    1359 KR: Humans, kagandi, and Sestomeran veytikka first came to Balioth, from the southern continent of Sestomera. They found small tribes of indigenous veytikka - to their surprise, speaking flawless Veyti despite living in more desperate conditions than even the veytikka of Sestomera. A continent-wide conquest began, with the humans and kagandi driving back the giants and their Gorgrom servants, and allying with the local veytikka tribes. The Cursewardens become widely recognized during this time, and induct many new members into their order.

    1359 KR

  • Founding of Chardecum

    1360 KR: The Founding of Chardecum: Colonists from Galkath, under the command of Tomas Silverwave, discover a ruined city and build a permanent settlement on its foundations. It is part of the province of Tyrema Ridge; Tyrema Ridge and Highsails are the two provinces that make up the domain of Southwind. Details of the initial exploration of the city are sparse, but according to legend the explorers plastered over and bricked up a number of discoveries.

    1360 KR

  • Gorgrom Incursion

    1366 KR: Gorgrom hordes come pouring out of the mountains of Tyrema Ridge. Only at the walls of the city are they finally turned back; half a dozen smaller inland settlements burn to the ground.

    1366 KR

  • Balioth Conquest Complete

    1385 KR: The conquest of Balioth is functionally complete, though it is so sparsely inhabited by the people of Sestomera that they cannot seriously be said to have explored it - something which remains true to the present day.

    1385 KR

  • Alchemist Society Chartered

    1411 KR: A group of kagandi alchemists receives a charter from the colonial governor of Adeschon to create the Society for Alchemical Refinement. They build their guildhall in the ruins of what must have once been a fantastically wealthy private residence (notably, it is sized for Gorgrom).

    1411 KR

  • Imperial Collapse

    1412 KR: The major powers of Sestomera fall to fighting among themselves. Over the next forty-eight years, the provinces are divided into fifteen domains.

    1412 KR

  • Prince of Adeschon Declared

    1416 KR: The colonial governer of Southwind, Lorien Silverwave, begins to style himself the Prince of Adeschon, following the abrupt end of Sestomeran support for colonies throughout Balioth.

    1416 KR

  • Saivel's Dual Crowns

    1416 KR: Saivel the Great of Rindaria attempts to ascend the throne of Ferradona as well. The Iron Temple opposes her, while the Sovereign Knights of the Council Fire support her. She gains the throne, and with the strength of the two domains combined, she begins her bloody wars of conquest, beginning with Eksoris and Dalassiria (who had formed an alliance against her).

    1416 KR

  • Sioctana in Green Discovered

    1420 KR: The massive statue known as Sioctana in Green is discovered in this year. The poor of Chardecum give offerings at the statue, a practice that continues to the present day.

    1420 KR

  • Eksoris Conquered

    1423 KR: Saivel conquers and annexes Eksoris. A significant resistance forms, known in the popular imagination as Tarimel's Defiance, even though (as far as anyone knows) there is no Tarimel. Saivel's chief advisor, Phoeras, counsels her to disestablish the school of wizardry and sorcery known as the Aumerine Manse, as the Society of the Wise represents a political body with a leadership that might turn against her hegemony at any change in leadership.

    1423 KR

  • Frosthallow Begins

    1429 KR: Dissolution and annihilation of the Aumerine Manse, and the beginning of the Frosthallow holiday as a time and place to hide from Saivel's agents and exchange arcane knowledge. The first Frosthallow is held on Sandpiper Island, as it is both pleasant in the winter and very far from Rindaria.

    1429 KR

  • War Reaches Tyrema Ridge

    1434 KR: The war that began as Tarimel's Defiance in Eksoris spreads throughout Balioth; in this year it reaches the province of Tyrema Ridge, which goes to war with its northern neighbor, the domain of Eldion. The exact causes of the war are unclear, but in the modern day, most Tyremans believe that the Grand Duke of Eldion, Noroth, was manipulated by the priests dwelling in the Abbey of the Immortal Saint.

    1434 KR

  • First Occupation

    1440 KR: The First Occupation: The war with Eldion is disastrous for both sides, but particularly for Tyrema Ridge, as a stateless army commanded by Master and Lady Sapphire seize Chardecum without a significant fight; without an army present, the city elders elect to open the gates to them. They sack the city, seizing hundreds of thousands of silver maravedis and an equal value of magical goods. A number of things bricked eighty years earlier are reopened, and innocent civilians in the city are forced to explore the passages and hand over everything they find. Shortly after, the arcane cabal known as the Gallant Shields enters into a secret alliance with the Society for Alchemical Refinement. Pooling their resources, they hire sellswords to bring about the overthrow of Master and Lady Sapphire.

    1440 KR

  • City Besieged

    1441 KR: Master and Lady Sapphire escape capture, fleeing the city and reuniting with their forces in the field. The coalition of sellswords, wizards, and alchemists begins to fray almost instantly. At the end of autumn of this year, Master and Lady Sapphire besiege the city, but are driven back by a renewed alliance. The mercenaries suffer catastrophic losses, however, and after the battle they pass into the direct control of the Gallant Shields.

    1441 KR

  • War of Red Sails Begins

    1442 KR: After negotiations with the Prince of Adeschon, Chardecum bends the knee in exchange for certain privileges for the Society for Alchemical Refinement and other guilds. The Gallant Shields become a chartered mercenary company, receiving letters of marque and reprisal for the duration of hostilities against Eldion. This begins the phase of the fighting known as the War of Red Sails.

    1442 KR

  • Maritime Blockade

    1443 KR: Eldion initiates a maritime blockade of Chardecum and Adeschon. The Prince of Adeschon is killed when his flagship sinks, and the Tyreman crown passes to his daughter Lilia.

    1443 KR

  • Diplomatic Scandal

    1449 KR: Eldion sends ambassadors to Chardecum to negotiate an end to the war. They die under mysterious circumstances. Investigations into the matter fail to exonerate the Princess of Adeschon's senior advisor, causing a scandal that inspires the Sovereign Knights of the Council Fire and other Kaldeshari factions to enter the war on Eldion's side.

    1449 KR

  • The Princess's Flight

    1453 KR Having seized the province of Sunrise Point, the Princess relocates to Grevanda, as it is considerably safer from the deployment of Eldenna forces at the time. Following a series of losses, she withdraws all defenders from Adeschon and Chardecum, leaving only irregular forces in Shale Pass and in the foothills. The Grand Duke of Eldion invests Adeschon and Chardecum at the end of the year.

    1453 KR

  • The Second Occupation Begins

    1454 KR The Second Occupation: The Grand Duke of Eldion names the Baronne Alexine Beaumarchais the provincial governor of occupied Tyrema Ridge. She is famously merciless, repeatedly crushing resistance cells almost immediately after their creation and publicly dismembering the families of all involved.

    1454 KR

  • The Second Occupation Ends

    1458 KR: The Second Occupation ends at last, as the Princess of Grevanda secures new allies on Sandpiper Island and blockades Chardecum by land and sea. Beaumarchais surrenders her colors and is held prisoner in Grevanda for the next three years.

    1458 KR

  • Peace Restored

    1460 KR: Peace is restored across Balioth after two generations of warfare.

    1460 KR

  • Congress of Lirane Concludes War

    1461 KR The Congress of Lirane formally ends the fighting that has ravaged Balioth. The Princess's claim to Tyrema Ridge is recognized over all others, and she breaks her promises to Master and Lady Sapphire (now the son and daughter-in-law of the ones who carried out the First Occupation). The Congress confirms Tyreman claims to the provinces of Sunrise Point and Sandpiper Island. Noroth becomes the first Grand Duke of Gallmonte.

    1461 KR

  • First Charter of the Gallant Shields Closes

    1473 KR The last of the three wizards who made up the original Gallant Shields cabal dies in a mission to explore the Sunken City of Adeirra. Due to legal complications, the Company's new charter is far less advantageous, and carries an annual fee.

    1473 KR

  • Death of Princess Lilia

    1494 KR The Princess Lilia dies at the age of 84, and is succeeded by Princess Rivay. Rivay is particularly well loved in Chardecum, as she eases the city's tax burden and reforms a corrupt bureaucracy.

    1494 KR

  • Plague of Sunrise Fever

    1497 KR: A plague known as sunrise fever (as it made sufferers especially sensitive to sunlight, and came from Sunrise Point) spreads into Chardecum. It affects humans, but not kagandi or veytikka.

    1497 KR

  • Alarra's Sail Rises

    1529 KR The infamous pirate Alarra begins her career in this year, capturing numerous merchant vessels near Chardecum and pillaging coastal settlements. Though the price on her head grows and grows, she never fails to evade Grevandan frigates.

    1529 KR

  • Navender Coup

    1536 KR: Kheryna carries out a coup d'etat against her father, the ruler of Navender. She exiles him to the Teeth, in Kaldeshar.

    1536 KR

  • Fallows of Chardecum Formed

    1537 KR The course of the Ollent River shifts, and what was once riverbed becomes the Fallows of Chardecum. It becomes home to the desperately poor.

    1537 KR

  • Princess Rivay Disappears

    1538 KR: Princess Rivay disappears under mysterious circumstances as part of a royal progress on Sandpiper Island. When she is not found for a year and a day, she is declared dead without heir, and noble electors pass the crown to Prince Adalar of the Montalvo family - the first human to rule Tyrema or Southwind since its founding.

    Var Dyrak drives the Solemn Watchers, a doomsday cult, from their tower in Merestine. He claims the tower for his own and begins inducting new recruits to the Tiger's Claw.

    1538 KR

  • Closing of the Eye & Strange Sails

    1539 KR: Annask Tioral destroys the Eye of Ychirra. Many beruch flee Erenn Kemesa, sailing eastward until they come to Balioth. They are treated with cautious respect.

    Beruch arrive in the port of Chardecum. Some of their ships, damaged in the voyage, have not left their moorings in the harbor since.

    1539 KR

  • First Parthé Created

    1545 KR: Parthala performs the ritual that creates the first Parthé. Many believe that she intends to use them to unseat Var Dyrak.

    1545 KR

  • Creation of the Stone of Torment

    1546 KR: Solomon Edrall creates the Stone of Torment. He and his servant Tagraz enter the Stone, and emerge again after seven days - having been transformed into a lich and a death knight, respectively.

    1546 KR

  • Rubycrest Guildhouse Completed

    1550 KR: Nianth completes construction of the Rubycrest Alchemists' Guildhouse, in Pereil. Nianth has not been seen since that day.

    1550 KR

  • Knightsgrave Uprising

    1552 KR: A significant peasant uprising in the province of Knightsgrave, in Berrona.

    1552 KR

  • Pillars Shaken

    1555 KR: The Prince of Grevanda exposes a Gallmontain spy, placed high within his court. This begins a long period of hostility, though not open warfare, with Gallmonte. Earthquakes are felt in every province of Tyrema in this year.

    1555 KR

  • Alarra's Final Raid

    1558 KR The last reported attack by Alarra the pirate takes place in this year, in which she captures and scuttles the Keening Wind, a ship owned by the Ironspar family.

    1558 KR

  • Moriscethe Isolated

    1564 KR: Beginning in the autumn, Morisceth became cut off from the rest of Dalassiria, as a result of the dangers of Shahar Passage.

    1564 KR

  • Campaign Founded

    1565 KR: The game begins on the first day of Talendos (April). Lady Mara Desponet is the Reeve. Significant noble families include Barrigo, Silvergrove, Ironspar, Goldhawk, Desponet, and Cabanellas. Chartered mercenary companies include the Gallant Shields and the Grand Escuderos.

    1565 KR

  • Lightning Crashes

    1566 KR Lady Mara resigns her post in protest of the Prince's and provincial governor's actions. A military governor arrives to invest the city. Later in the year, the angel Douma is slain in Count Emrion's Plaza and the military occupation is driven out.

    1566 KR

  • Present Day

    1567 KR: Present day. Vyrion Silvercrow is the Reeve. The Council of Chardecum continues to debate and refine plans for a new, post-revolutionary city and the election of a new reeve. The fortunes of the Silvergrove family go into steep decline, and the Goldhawk family's holdings in the city have been seized. Lady Mara Desponet has asserted the title of Provincial Governor, and Lord Kolus Goldhawk has remained safely in Grevanda.

    The Green Man restored upon Midsummer by allies in the Gallant Shields.

    The neighboring domain of Gallmonte has suffered a demonic invasion for some months. The city of Adeschon, west of Chardecum, has joined the revolutionary cause against the Prince.

    Domixalra the Black-Bronze awakens and asserts claims upon Sunrise Point and Tyrema Ridge, with a pledge fealty/loyalty from Rhegharadon the Red-Gold and his Scarlet Knights, and Chisise the storm giant.

    1567 KR
